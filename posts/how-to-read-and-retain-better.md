Why do people remember things better in school? Is it because they're smarter? Mostly - they had better motivation and technique to retain the information they read. They were more engage.

We'll explore a technique used a technique called Marginilia technique. It would help you as a reader better learn.


Outline:
- Marginilia technique(Summary: 1, 1, 2, 3, 4 ...) : 
 1. Read first paragraph, summarize paragraph to one sentece on the margins. 
 2. Read second paragraph, summarize second paragraph in one sentence.
 3. Read 3rd paragraph, summarize first and second paragraphs into one sentence then summarize the second paragraph. In total - there will be 2 sentences. 1 for paragraphs 1 and 2 and then 2nd paragraph for 2.
 4. 4th paragraph: Summarize 1, 2, 3 paragraphs into 1. Summarize the current paragraph. 
 5. 5th paragraph: Summarize 1, 2, 3, 4 parapgraphs into 1. Summarize the current paragraph.
