## Appendix: Methodology

**Research approach** — Data compiled from publicly available sources current as of August 2026. Primary sources included official center and company websites, leadership/founder pages, and institutional or corporate announcements. Funding figures reflect only publicly disclosed amounts and are approximate.

**Categorization** — Primary: institutional type. Secondary: geography. Tertiary: technical platform (trapped-ion, superconducting, neutral-atom, photonic, etc.). Founding year added for timeline context.

**Scope** — Emphasizes named, trackable centers and the most visible pure-play quantum startups. Big Tech internal efforts (IBM Quantum, Google Quantum AI) noted in context but not listed. Living snapshot intended for quarterly updates.
