Amongst my friends - I've been labeled as someone on the austistic spectrum. While I do not agree with it - I tend to have social dynamic challenges which can create awkwardness: I have a hard time reading peoples emotions, I cannot comprehend peoples expressions/tones, I cannot read subtexts. 

These are advice that I wish I was given to create better relationships with a stranger for the first time.


## Goal: Stranger -> Friends
The goal of any first interaction is to move from strangers to friends. To do that one needs to gradually build trust within that time frame of conversations and move on to establishing friendship.
